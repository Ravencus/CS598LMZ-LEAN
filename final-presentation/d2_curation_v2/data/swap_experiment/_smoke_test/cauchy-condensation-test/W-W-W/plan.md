1. Prove the dyadic block estimate: for every `k`, the sum of the terms `a_n` with `2^k ≤ n < 2^(k+1)` is squeezed between `2^k · a(2^(k+1))` and `2^k · a(2^k)`, using monotonicity and nonnegativity.

2. Show that the original series `∑_{n≥1} a_n` converges exactly when the series of dyadic block sums `∑_k (∑_{2^k ≤ n < 2^(k+1)} a_n)` converges.

3. Compare the dyadic block-sum series with the condensation series `∑_k 2^k a(2^k)` by using the block estimate and a one-step index shift on the lower bound.

4. Deduce each summability implication separately from the comparison bounds: convergence of `∑_k 2^k a(2^k)` implies convergence of the block-sum series, and conversely convergence of the block-sum series implies convergence of `∑_k 2^k a(2^k)`.

5. Combine the equivalence between the original series and the block-sum series with the equivalence between the block-sum series and the condensation series to obtain the final “if and only if” statement.

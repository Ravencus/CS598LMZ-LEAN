1. Show that as the threshold `a` increases, the superlevel sets `{x | |f x| > a}` only shrink, so `a ↦ λ_f(a)` is antitone.

2. Prove right-continuity of `a ↦ λ_f(a)` by identifying the intersection of the sets `{x | |f x| > b}` for `b > a` with `{x | |f x| > a}` and applying continuity of measure from above.

3. Prove that a pointwise bound `|f x| ≤ |g x|` forces inclusion `{x | |f x| > a} ⊆ {x | |g x| > a}` for every `a`, giving `λ_f(a) ≤ λ_g(a)`.

4. For an increasing family `|F n x| ↑ |f x|`, show first that for each fixed `a` the functions `n ↦ λ_{F n}(a)` are monotone, then identify their pointwise limit using the union of the superlevel sets to obtain `λ_{F n}(a) ↑ λ_f(a)`.

5. Prove the sum bound by reducing it to the set inclusion `{x | |g(x) + h(x)| > a} ⊆ {x | |g(x)| > a/2} ∪ {x | |h(x)| > a/2}` via the triangle inequality.

6. Prove the product bound by reducing it to the set inclusion `{x | |g(x)h(x)| > a} ⊆ {x | |g(x)| > √(a/2)} ∪ {x | |h(x)| > √(a/2)}` using the contrapositive of `|g(x)| ≤ √(a/2)` and `|h(x)| ≤ √(a/2)`.

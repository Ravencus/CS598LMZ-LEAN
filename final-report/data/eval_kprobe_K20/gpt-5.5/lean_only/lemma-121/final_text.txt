The theorem is false as stated: ordinary complex polynomials in `z` are not uniformly dense in `C(𝕋, ℂ)`. A standard counterexample is `f z = conj z`; uniform limits of polynomials in `z` on the unit circle extend holomorphically to the disk, but `conj z` does not.

The correct dense class would be Laurent polynomials, or equivalently polynomials in both `z` and `conj z` / the star-subalgebra generated by `z`.

- sum-estimates-via-summation-by-parts-hub: For `z ≠ 1`, convergence is naturally proved by Abel/summation-by-parts estimates using bounded geometric partial sums of `z^n` on each square-root block.
- piecewise-estimates-hub: The proof should split into structurally different cases, especially `z = 1` versus `z ≠ 1`, and also split the series into blocks where `⌊√n⌋` is constant.
- term-by-term-estimates-hub: Within each block, one compares `1/n` to simpler quantities such as `1/k^2` and controls the resulting error term by elementary per-term bounds.
- integral-estimates-for-sums-hub: For the `z = 1` case, the block sums `∑_{k^2 ≤ n < (k+1)^2} 1/n` need standard sum-versus-integral/log estimates to show they behave like `2/k`, giving an alternating-series/Dirichlet-type convergence argument.

The theorem states that any continuous function on the unit circle can be uniformly approximated by **complex polynomials in z** (not in z and z̄). However, this statement is **mathematically false**. For example, f(z) = z̄ on the unit circle cannot be uniformly approximated by polynomials in z — the L² inner product ⟨z̄, z^n⟩ = 0 for all n ≥ 0, so for any polynomial P, sup|z|=1 |z̄ - P(z)| ≥ ‖z̄‖_L² = 1.

Stone-Weierstrass requires the algebra to separate points AND be closed under conjugation (for ℂ-valued functions), but z alone doesn't generate a self-conjugate algebra on S¹.

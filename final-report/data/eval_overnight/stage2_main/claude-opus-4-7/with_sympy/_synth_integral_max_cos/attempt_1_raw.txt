Looking at this problem, I need to prove that ∫₀^{2π} max(cos u, 0) du = 2.

The integrand is the positive part of cos u. Over [0, 2π], cos u ≥ 0 on [0, π/2] ∪ [3π/2, 2π], and cos u ≤ 0 on [π/2, 3π/2]. So:
- ∫₀^{π/2} cos u du = sin(π/2) - sin(0) = 1
- ∫_{π/2}^{3π/2} 0 du = 0  
- ∫_{3π/2}^{2π} cos u du = sin(2π) - sin(3π/2) = 0 - (-1) = 1

Total = 2.
